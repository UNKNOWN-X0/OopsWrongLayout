Spelling rules

Doubled consonants become a small tsu (kitte → きって). ん is used before a consonant or at the end of a word; type nn or n' for an explicit ん before a vowel. In katakana, repeated vowels contract into the long-vowel mark (kyuuto → キュート). konnichiwa and konbanwa are spelled with は, matching real usage.

Why no pinyin → hanzi

Unlike kana, pinyin syllables map to many unrelated characters (ma → 妈/麻/马/骂), so it needs a dictionary and a candidate picker rather than a direct conversion.
